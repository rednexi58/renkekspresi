### Description (English)

Welcome aboard Color Express! Colorful trains are rolling down the tracks. Send the right wagons to the station, use your slots wisely and collect every car before the train reaches the end of the line.

HOW TO PLAY
• Tap a wagon whose arrow path is clear.
• The wagon drives to a station slot and collects the train cars of its color.
• Station slots are limited, so plan your order!
• If the train reaches the end you still have 2 more chances; the third time the level is over.

FEATURES
• 200 carefully crafted levels, with double and triple train challenges every 3 levels
• A world tour through summer and winter themed cities
• Freeze, Blast and Undo boosters
• Combo rewards, a star chest and a daily gift
• Collectible locomotive skins
• Calm music and relaxing gameplay

VIP MEMBERSHIP
VIP is a monthly auto-renewable subscription. One extra station slot is always open in every level and no ads are shown between levels. Payment is charged to your Apple Account when the purchase is confirmed. The subscription renews automatically at the same price unless it is cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the current period. You can manage or cancel your subscription at any time in your App Store account settings.
